Questions

Good to know.

How much does a commission cost?

Commissions start at $500. The final price depends on size, framing, and whether we source materials or work with pieces you already own. You get a quote before any work begins.

Can you use items I already own?

Yes. Stickers, magazines, tickets, and other pieces from your collection can become the artwork itself.

Is everything on the site in stock?

No. The shop shows confirmed listings. The private collection and sticker reference library show what exists, not what is for sale. Send a request and we will confirm whether a piece is available or can be sourced.

Can you find something specific?

Often, yes. Share the item, a photo, a budget, or even a memory of it, and we will use our collection and network to look. Request access →
